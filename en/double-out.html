DARTS RULES

What is Double Out?

Double Out means a leg only ends when the final dart takes the remaining score to zero by hitting a double or BULL.

The key rule

Scoring exactly the remaining points is not enough. The final dart must be D1–D20 or BULL; otherwise the visit is a BUST.

What causes a BUST?

Going below zero

If the visit takes the remaining score below zero, the entire visit is cancelled.

Leaving 1

A score of 1 cannot be finished with a double, so the visit is a BUST.

Zero without a double

Reaching zero with a single or treble does not finish the leg in Double Out.

Can BULL finish a leg?

Yes. The inner BULL scores 50 and counts as double 25. That means 50 can be finished with one BULL, while 170 can be finished T20 → T20 → BULL.
