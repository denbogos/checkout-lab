Dartboard height and distance

How to hang a dartboard at home or in a club: height, throw distance and the diagonal check. All figures follow WDF and PDC standards for steel-tip darts.

Key numbers

1.73 m / 5 ft 8 in — floor to bullseye.
2.37 m / 7 ft 9¼ in — board face to oche.
2.93 m / 9 ft 7⅜ in — bullseye to oche diagonally.

How to hang a dartboard

1. Height

The centre of the bull sits exactly 1.73 m (5 ft 8 in) above the floor, with 20 at the top.

2. Distance

The oche (throw line) is 2.37 m (7 ft 9¼ in) from the face of the board, measured along the floor.

3. Check

Measure from the bullseye to the back of the oche: it should be 2.93 m (9 ft 7⅜ in).

Standard board dimensions

Board diameter

451 mm (17¾ in); the scoring area is a 170 mm radius to the outside of the double ring.

Rings

Double and treble rings are about 8 mm wide. The outer edge of the treble ring is 107 mm from the centre.

Bull and outer bull

The bullseye (50) is 12.7 mm across, the outer bull (25) 31.8 mm.

Soft-tip darts

Electronic boards usually use 2.44 m (8 ft) to the oche, with the same 1.73 m height.

Tips for home

Choose a wall with no doors nearby and fit a surround or mat — misses happen. Light the board from above and the side so darts do not cast shadows on the segments. Keep 20 at the top, so the checkout table routes match your board.

FAQ

How high should a dartboard be?

The centre of the bullseye is 1.73 m (5 ft 8 in) from the floor.

How far is the oche from the dartboard?

2.37 m (7 ft 9¼ in) from the board face for steel-tip darts; usually 2.44 m (8 ft) for soft-tip.

How do I check the board is hung correctly?

The diagonal from the bullseye to the oche should be 2.93 m (9 ft 7⅜ in).

What size is a standard dartboard?

A bristle board is 451 mm (17¾ in) across.
