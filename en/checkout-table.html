How to read routes

T20 — treble 20 (60 points), D16 — double 16 (32 points), S20 — single 20, BULL — inner Bull (50 points). The arrow shows the dart order.

What is Double Out?

In Double Out, a leg ends only when the final dart hits a double. BULL counts as double 25 and can also finish a leg. The highest three-dart checkout is 170: T20 → T20 → BULL.

Why can’t 169 be checked out?

169 is a bogey number: there is no valid three-dart route that finishes on a double.

How do I find a score?

Enter the number in the search field above. The table will immediately show that score and its checkout options.

Is there only one route?

No. Many scores have several valid routes. Checkout Lab shows a primary route and several alternatives.
