Online darts scorer

Checkout Lab is a free online darts score keeper for phone and desktop. Enter each visit and it subtracts the score, flags busts, shows the Double Out checkout route and keeps your stats: three-dart average, 180s and best checkouts.

No sign-up and no ads, and after the first visit it works offline. Install it on your phone like an app.

What Checkout Lab does

  • 501 and 301 scoring — with checkout hints, sets and Double In
  • Cricket — marks board for 15–20 and Bull, MPR
  • Party games — Shanghai, Killer, Around the Clock
  • Practice — Bob’s 27, 121 Checkout, doubles
  • Tournaments — knockout or round robin for 3–16 players
  • Checkout table — every route from 2 to 170

FAQ

How do I keep score in darts online?

Open Checkout Lab, pick 501 or 301, add players and enter each visit total. The remaining score, busts and checkout routes are calculated for you.

Can I play against the computer?

Yes — 301/501 and Cricket have a computer opponent with five levels, from beginner to pro.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After the first visit the site is stored on your device and works offline; install it on your phone like an app.

How much does it cost?

Checkout Lab is completely free and ad-free.
