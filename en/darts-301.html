301 in short

Players start on 301 and reduce the score each visit. In Double Out, zero only counts when the final dart lands in a double or BULL.

How 301 differs from 501

The scoring rules are the same, but the lower starting score means players reach checkout range much sooner. It is useful for quick matches and practicing finishes.

Shorter legs

Less time building a score and more decisions around Double Out and preferred doubles.

Automatic BUST handling

Going below zero, leaving 1, or reaching zero without a double restores the score from before the visit.

301 scoring example

301− 100 →201− 101 →100→ T20 → D20

From 100, a standard route is T20 → D20. If the first dart misses the treble, Checkout Lab can show another route.

Useful for checkout practice

301 puts you into the 40–170 range more often, making it a good format for practicing finishes under match conditions. Use the checkout calculator for isolated finish practice.
