How to use it

Enter your current remaining score. T means treble, D means double, S means single, and BULL is 50 points.

Example: 121 → T20 → T11 → D14

How the calculator works

Checkout Lab uses standard Double Out routes: the final dart must hit a double or BULL. Many scores have several valid solutions, so the calculator can show a primary route and alternatives.

2–60

Many scores can be finished in one or two darts, often aiming to leave D20, D16 or another preferred double.

61–170

Routes usually begin with a treble. When two trebles are used, Checkout Lab lists the higher treble first.

Why can’t some scores be checked out?

169, 168, 166, 165, 163, 162 and 159 cannot be finished in three darts under Double Out. These are known as bogey numbers, so the goal is to set up the next visit.
